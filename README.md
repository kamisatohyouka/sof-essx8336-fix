# Making sure your laptop is not a mute.
A repository for fixing Audio in a laptop or computer with Intel Celeron/Pentium that has Everest Semiconductor ES8336 I2S Codec in it.

Most of people struggling to fix their laptop after they reinstalled their Windows, or if they migrated to Linux Distros because of the infamous codec essx8336.
